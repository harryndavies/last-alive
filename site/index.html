Never played? Here’s the idea.

🕵️

Everyone is a hunter

You’re secretly given one person to “get”. Someone else has secretly been given you. Nobody knows who.

📆

It plays out for days

There’s no board and no turns. The game runs in the background of normal life: at the office, at the pub, on holiday, at home.

🦆

Nobody actually gets hurt

A “kill” is getting them to take something from you (a pen, a biscuit, a rubber chicken, anything) in the right place, followed by the right words.

You might know it as Assassin, Killer or Gotcha. Last Alive is that game, with your phone doing the secret-keeping and the admin.

Setting up a game

  1. 1

    Round up 4 or more friends

    One person starts a game and gets a code like CLUE-7Q. Everyone else joins with the code (or a shared link), a name and a colour. That’s it, no accounts.

  2. 2

    Everyone writes three cards

    A weapon (anything you can hand someone), a place and a phrase. These go into a shared deck. Make them fun, not impossible.

  3. 3

    The app deals secret missions

    Everyone is placed in a hidden circle and each person hunts the next. Each hunter gets one target plus a weapon, place and phrase from someone else’s cards. Never your own.

  4. 4

    The hunt is on

    Check your mission, put your phone away and act normal. Very, very normal.

Reading your mission

Your mission has four parts. All four have to line up.

Target
The one person you’re after. Keep it to yourself.
Weapon
Get your target to take this object from you. They have to take it willingly, so be smooth: “Hold this a sec?”
Place
It only counts if it happens here.
Phrase
Once it’s in their hand, say this out loud to them. Then it’s done.

Making a kill

  1. 01

    Hand it over

    Get them to take your weapon from you, in your place.

  2. 02

    Say the phrase

    To their face. That’s the moment they know.

  3. 03

    Snap a photo

    Optional, but it goes in the kill cam, and it helps if they argue.

  4. 04

    Tap “I got them”

    Their phone lights up: “Someone says they got you.”

“Yes, I’m dead”

They’re out, and everyone sees it in the feed.

No answer in 12 hours

Silence counts as yes. No hiding from your phone.

You take their mission

When you get someone, you inherit whatever they were working on: their target, their weapon, their place and their phrase.

So the circle keeps closing. Every kill makes the remaining hunters more dangerous, and the person hunting you just got one step closer.

Ana hunts Ben, Ben hunts Cat, and so on round the circle.

Last one standing wins

When it’s down to one, the game’s over. Then everyone watches the kill cam: a replay of every kill, with the photos, the weapons and the phrases, in the order they happened.

  • ✕Sam got Priya with a wooden spoon in the lift. “Nothing personal.”
  • ✕Jo got Sam with a Post-it on the bus. “Sweet dreams.”
  • ♛Jo is the last one alive!

House rules we recommend

The app keeps the secrets. Keeping it fun is up to you. Agree these before you start.

Hand to hand

It only counts if they take it from you willingly. No forcing it on them, no throwing it, no sneaking it into their pocket or bag.

Safe zones

Pick places and times that are off-limits: work meetings, driving, the gym, exams. If it would get someone in trouble, it doesn’t count.

Only players count

Leave strangers, colleagues who aren’t playing and members of the public out of it. No sneaking into places you shouldn’t be.

Be a good victim

If you got got, own it. Tap “Yes, I’m dead” and enjoy the show from the sidelines.

Ask before you post

Kill photos are private to your game. If you want to share one beyond it, ask the person in it first.

The host decides

For anything the rules don’t cover, the host makes the call. Their word is final. Bribes are a matter for your conscience.

Questions

How many people do we need?

At least 4. It gets better with more. Offices, flats, friend groups and holidays of 8 to 20 are ideal.

How long does a game last?

Usually a few days to a couple of weeks. It depends on how many play, how often you see each other and how paranoid everyone gets.

Can we give it an end date, like for a holiday?

Yes. The host can set an “end by” time. Everyone sees the countdown, and the last 24 hours are sudden death: anyone can get anyone, with any weapon, in any place. When time runs out, the survivor with the most kills wins. The host can extend the game, but never cut it short.

Do I need to download anything or make an account?

No. It runs in your phone’s browser. You join with a code and a name. You can add it to your home screen if you want it to feel like an app.

What if someone says my kill didn’t count?

It goes to the host, who sees your claim and any photo and makes the call. If they rule it out, you keep your mission and try again.

What if I lose my phone?

When you join you’re shown a 4-digit PIN. On a new phone, join with the same name and that PIN, and your seat comes back.

Can someone dodge by never opening the app?

No. If they don’t answer a claim within 12 hours, it counts. If they leave the game while someone has a claim on them, that kill counts too.

What happens to our photos?

Kill photos and profile photos are only visible to players in your game. They’re deleted, along with the rest of the game, 7 days after it ends.

Can we go again straight away?

Yes. Tap Rematch on the winner screen. Everyone else gets a one-tap “Join the rematch” with the same names, and the app deals new missions.

Does it cost anything?

No. It’s free.
