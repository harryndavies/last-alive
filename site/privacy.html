Privacy

Short version: we keep as little as we can, for as short as we can.

No accounts

You never give us an email address, phone number or password. When you open the app, your browser gets an anonymous ID. That ID is how the app recognises your seat in a game.

What a game stores

  • The display name and colour you pick.
  • The weapon, place and phrase cards you write.
  • Who hunts whom, and every kill claim: who, when, and any note you add.
  • Kill photos, if you choose to take one.
  • A scrambled (hashed) copy of your 4-digit PIN, used only to give you your seat back on a new phone.

Who can see what

  • Only players in your game can see that game. Strangers can’t browse games.
  • Your mission is visible to you alone.
  • A kill photo is visible to the killer and the victim, and to the host if the claim is disputed. Once the kill is confirmed, everyone in the game can see it.

When it’s deleted

Everything about a game, photos included, is deleted 7 days after the game ends. Games that never start are deleted after 30 days.

Where it lives

Game data and photos are stored with Supabase in London (UK). The app itself is served by Vercel. Like most websites, they keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for a short time for security. We don’t use advertising or tracking cookies, and we don’t sell or share your data.
